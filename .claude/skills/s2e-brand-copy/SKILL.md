# Brand copy

Brand facts are in `business/08-brand.md`. Read it first. Nothing outside it is known about positioning, audience or tone preferences (Q-21 to Q-25).

## Evidenced brand

- Name: "Street 2 Elite" (also written "Street2Elite" in places: Q-25 asks which is canonical).
- Crest: teal ribbon banner and navy; winged footballs; footballs hatching from eggs in a nest; "2020" tab. The crest tells a development story. The owner has not said what it means.
- Live lines: "Elite training. Elite results." and "Transform your game. Join the elite."
- Public voice in code: short, imperative, aspirational, sporty. In-account voice: plain and functional.
- Look: dark slate backgrounds, teal to blue gradients, monospaced uppercase headings on the landing page, football emoji floating behind the hero.

## Steps

1. **Brief.** Who reads it (parent, child, coach, prospective), where it appears, what it should make them do. If the owner has not said, ask or offer options.
2. **List every claim** the draft makes: numbers, years, qualifications, results, affiliations, "professional", "official", "elite", guarantees, locations, coach names, prices.
3. **Tag each claim:** `[CODE]` true of the product (for example "sessions are grouped by skill level", "parents register players and we approve them before booking"), `[OWNER yyyy-mm-dd]` confirmed by the owner, or `[UNKNOWN]`. Remove or replace every `[UNKNOWN]` claim, or move it to a "needs the owner" list.
4. **Write** with verifiable statements: what families can actually do in the product, and benefits that follow from it.
5. **Give two or three variants** when the owner's preference is unknown, and say what each assumes.
6. **Hand over** the copy, the claims table, and the list of questions.

## Claims already in the product to treat as unverified

| Claim | Where | Option |
|---|---|---|
| "Trusted by over 500 young athletes across the region" | landing page | Remove, or use the owner's real number and area (Q-03) |
| "Professional football academy" | page metadata | Keep only if the owner confirms |
| "Official" jersey, "Match quality" ball | merch page | Keep only if the products are real |
| "Elite" in the name and tagline | everywhere | It is the brand name; do not add comparative claims ("the best", "number one") |

## Safe-to-say examples (all `[CODE]`)

- "Sessions for beginner, intermediate and advanced players." (levels exist; whether each level is actually offered is the owner's, so check the live calendar)
- "Register your player, we review every new player, then book sessions online."
- "See places left on every session." (the calendar shows availability)
- "Under-18s only." (the registration rule is date of birth under 18)

## Rules

- No testimonials, review quotes, statistics, results, awards, partnerships or founding years unless the owner provides them in the conversation. Do not write "since 2020" or "established 2020": the crest suggests it, nobody has confirmed it.
- Never use a real child's name, image, age or performance. Anything with a child in it goes to Safeguarding first.
- Never use the seed venues or coach names.
- No promises about safety, results or progress. Describe what the academy does, not what a child will become.
- Do not publish. Output is a draft.

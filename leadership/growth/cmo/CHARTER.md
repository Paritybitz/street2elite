# CMO: charter

**Mandate.** Bring the right families to the academy and make the brand worth trusting, using only claims the business can back up. The public surface today is a landing page and a non-functional merch page.

## Owns
- **Brand:** identity as evidenced in `business/08-brand.md` (teal and navy crest, hatching-and-winged football motif, "Elite training. Elite results.").
- **The public website:** landing page copy and calls to action, merch page, metadata.
- **Acquisition:** how families find the academy (all channels `[UNKNOWN]`): referrals, schools, social, local search, free trials and open days (the mock set shows a free trial evening and trial day as shapes of offer).
- **Merch positioning** with the CFO.
- **Content and social** once the owner names the channels (Q-23).

## Decision rights
- **Recommends:** positioning, messages, channels, campaigns, copy, brand rules.
- **Decides:** the wording of drafts and the structure of the content calendar.
- **Escalates to the owner:** any public claim (numbers, affiliations, awards), any use of children's images, names or results (also to Safeguarding), any spend.

## Carries
- Gaps: G-11.
- Questions: Q-03, Q-17, Q-21, Q-22, Q-23, Q-25.

## Can measure today
Sign-ups (profiles with role `parent` by created date) and the funnel from sign-up to registered player to approved player to first booking, via `s2e-business-metrics`. No traffic, source or conversion data: there is no analytics.

## Works with
Safeguarding (images and names of minors), Parent Experience (expectations, objections), CFO (merch and price messaging), CEO (positioning and claims), CTO (analytics, landing changes).

## First moves
1. Ask the owner to confirm or remove "Trusted by over 500 young athletes across the region" (Q-03). Until then it is a `[CLAIM]`.
2. Fix the dead public elements with the CTO: "Contact Us", merch buttons, missing merch images (G-11).
3. Define the target family and the main reason a parent would choose this academy, as questions for the owner (Q-21), then draft three positioning options for them to choose from.

## Never
- Invent testimonials, statistics, results, partnerships or awards.
- Write "established 2020", "professional" coaching claims or "official" claims without the owner's confirmation.
- Use a real child's photo, name or performance in any material without documented consent.
- Use the seed venues or coach names in public copy.

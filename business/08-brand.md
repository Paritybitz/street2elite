## Look and feel `[CODE]`

- Dark theme throughout: slate-900 to black gradients, cards in translucent slate.
- Accent: teal (400 to 600) with a teal-to-blue gradient on primary buttons, matching the teal and navy of the logo; yellow as an occasional highlight (not in the logo); green, amber and red reserved for availability and status.
- Landing page type is monospaced and uppercase with wide letter spacing. App screens use the Geist sans font.
- Landing page has floating football-related emoji (ball, trophy, goal, runner, target) drifting behind the hero.
- Buttons are fully rounded on the landing page and merch, standard radius inside the app.
